OUR ESSENCE & HEART

A Vibrant Family Church of Uncompromising Excellence

Christ Embassy New Benin is a warm, royal sanctuary committed to helping men, women, and youths uncover the richness of God’s Word, mature in faith, fulfill their divine purpose, and positively impact their cities and nations.

Under the inspirational leadership and pastoral mantle of Pastor Joseph Atibi-Brown, thousands have stepped into their kingly inheritance in Christ. We are not just a congregation; we are anointed dispensers of God’s divine life, radiating kingdom virtues in our homes, workplaces, and communities.
